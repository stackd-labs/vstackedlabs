# Stackd Studios — Positioning & Messaging Guide

> The single source of truth for site copy. When in doubt, write to this.

## What we are

**An operations system studio.** We build the custom operational software and
automations a business actually runs on — and turn the best of those builds into
products of our own.

We are **not** an "AI agency." AI is *how* we build fast; it is never *what* we sell.

## The three pillars

| Pillar | What it means | Proof |
|---|---|---|
| **Solutions** | Custom software built for how your business actually works — intake, scheduling, billing, portals, dashboards. | Client builds across studios, clinics, nonprofits, local service. |
| **Automation** | The manual, repetitive work wired to run itself. | Workflows shipped end-to-end. |
| **Venture Studio** | We design, build, and launch our own products — and bring that product muscle to your build. | FlowCore, RinkSync, our own SaaS. |

## Voice rules

**Lead with the system / the outcome. Bury the technology.**

- ✅ Say: *systems, custom software, automations, workflows, dashboards, portals,
  products, the thing that runs itself, built, shipped, runs on its own.*
- ❌ Avoid: *AI-powered, powered by AI, intelligent systems, leverage AI, unlock,
  harness, supercharge, cutting-edge, revolutionary.*
- "AI" may appear **lowercase, as a method, secondary** — never in a headline.
- Concrete > abstract. Name the actual system (a booking flow, an intake agent,
  a billing automation), not "an AI solution."

## Taglines

- Primary: **"The system your business runs on."**
- Badge / descriptor: **"Solutions · Automation · Venture Studio"**
- Supporting: *"Custom systems. Built to run themselves."*
